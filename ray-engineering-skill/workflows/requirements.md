# Requirements Workflow

## Objective

Convert an idea into testable requirements.

## Process

1. Restate the problem.
2. Identify users/actors.
3. Identify primary workflows.
4. Define functional requirements.
5. Define non-functional requirements.
6. Define constraints.
7. Define non-goals.
8. Identify assumptions.
9. Define acceptance criteria.
10. Surface unresolved questions.
11. Obtain human confirmation.

## Rule

Do not turn assumptions into requirements without confirmation.
